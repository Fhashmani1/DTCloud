Why MDR vs. Traditional Security

What Makes MDR Different from Traditional Security

Traditional antivirus software and basic firewalls are designed mainly to stop known threats. Modern attackers often use techniques — stolen credentials, fileless malware, legitimate admin tools — that can slip past those defenses.

Managed detection and response adds proactive threat hunting, behavioral analysis, and real-time response to fill those gaps.

Rather than waiting for alerts, analysts look for signs of threats hiding in your environment, investigate suspicious behavior, and act when an attack is confirmed.

  • Proactive threat hunting — not just reactive alert management
  • Behavioral analytics help detect unknown and fileless attacks
  • Human analysts review alerts to reduce false-positive noise
  • Shifts your security posture from reactive to proactive

Our Process

How Our MDR Service Works

From onboarding to ongoing protection — here's what you can expect when you partner with DTCloudOn for MDR.

1

Discovery & Onboarding

We assess your environment, map your assets, and deploy monitoring agents on a schedule planned to minimize disruption.

2

Baseline & Tuning

Analysts establish a behavioral baseline for your environment and tune detection rules to reduce noise and false positives.

3

Continuous Monitoring

Your environment is monitored around the clock, with significant alerts reviewed by human analysts — not left to automated tools alone.

4

Threat Response

When a real threat is confirmed, response actions begin — isolating affected systems, blocking malicious activity, and notifying your team.

5

Investigation & Recovery

We investigate the root cause, identify how the attacker got in, and guide remediation to help prevent recurrence.

6

Reporting & Improvement

Regular reports with threat summaries and actionable recommendations help you keep improving your security posture.
