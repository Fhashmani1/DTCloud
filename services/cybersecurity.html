The Threat Landscape

Common Cyber Threats Facing Businesses Today

Phishing

Deceptive emails and messages remain one of the most common ways attackers gain an initial foothold

Ransomware

Attacks that encrypt or steal data can halt operations — making prevention and tested backups essential

Stolen Credentials

Compromised passwords give attackers legitimate-looking access, which MFA and monitoring help limit

Email Fraud

Business email compromise targets payments and sensitive data by impersonating trusted contacts
