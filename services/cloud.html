Cloud Benefits

Cloud Solutions for Today's Business —
and Tomorrow's Office

Once a confusing concept, today the benefits of the cloud are crystal clear. If you host virtual meetings for remote employees, collaborate on shared files, or check email on your phone — you're already using the cloud.

📁 Document Management

Gone are the days when file management meant locking material behind a physical office door. DTCloudOn can help with smart permission controls to monitor user access to data from anywhere.

📈 Scalability

Selecting the right cloud solution is challenging. DTCloudOn can help with design-and-build services that are right-sized for your business, and easy to scale up or down depending on usage and budget.

⚡ Productivity & Efficiency

With the cloud, collaboration is streamlined: employees in the office and in the field can join a presentation together and work off the same document in real-time, while distributed teams fulfill their responsibilities.

💰 Cost-Effective Computing

Cloud services can reduce some hardware and maintenance expenses while introducing usage-based charges. We help evaluate licensing, migration costs, and operating expenses before recommending a solution.

📱 Increased Mobility

With the right access policies, cloud applications and approved data can be available to authorized employees across locations, helping support hybrid and remote work.

🛡️ Better Prepared for Disaster

Cloud resilience depends on the architecture and backup configuration. We help plan off-site backups, redundancy, and documented recovery procedures for disruptive events.

Deployment Options

Choose Your Cloud Model

Every business has different needs. DTCloudOn helps you choose the right cloud model — or a combination — to meet your goals.

🌐

Public Cloud

Hosted by providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud — scalable, cost-effective, and ideal for businesses that need flexibility without managing hardware.

🏢

Private Cloud

Dedicated cloud infrastructure can provide additional control and customization for organizations with specific workload or security requirements.

🔀

Hybrid Cloud

The best of both worlds — keep sensitive workloads on private infrastructure while leveraging public cloud for scalability, cost savings, and flexibility.

Our Process

How DTCloudOn Migrates Your Business to the Cloud

A structured cloud migration approach, from assessment and design through testing and optimization, with disruption and security risks addressed during planning.

1

Cloud Readiness Assessment

We evaluate your current infrastructure, applications, and data to identify what moves to the cloud, what stays on-premises, and what gets modernized.

2

Architecture Design

We design a cloud architecture tailored to your security, compliance, performance, and budget requirements — with a platform recommendation and cost estimate.

3

Migration Planning

A detailed migration plan with phasing, timelines, rollback procedures, and communication plans — so your team knows exactly what to expect.

4

Secure Migration

We schedule and carry out migration activities according to an agreed plan, test workloads, and validate access and functionality before final cutover.

5

6

Ongoing Optimization

Ongoing management options include usage reviews, cost optimization, maintenance, and security checks based on the services selected.
