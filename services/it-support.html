Our Process

How DTCloudOn IT Support Works

Simple to engage, focused on resolution — here's what happens from the moment you reach out.

1

Submit a Request

Call, email, or chat — whatever works for you. We make it easy to reach us the way you prefer.

2

Triage

Your issue is categorized by severity and routed to the right technician — no bouncing around between teams.

3

Remote Resolution

Many issues can be resolved remotely using secure screen sharing and remote access tools.

4

Onsite Visit (if needed)

When remote resolution isn't possible, we arrange onsite assistance at your location.

5

Resolution & Documentation

Each ticket is documented — what happened, what was done, and how to prevent it — so knowledge builds over time.

6

Follow-Up & Prevention

We follow up to confirm the issue is resolved and review ticket patterns to help prevent repeat problems.
