Our Process

How DTCloudOn IT Support Works

Simple to engage, fast to resolve — here's what happens from the moment you reach out.

1

Submit a Ticket

Call, email, chat, or use our self-service portal — whatever works for you. We make it easy to reach us the way you prefer.

2

Instant Triage

Your issue is immediately categorized by severity and routed to the right technician for the job — no bouncing around between teams.

3

Remote Resolution

Most issues are resolved remotely within minutes. Our technicians use secure screen sharing and remote access tools to fix problems fast.

4

On-Site Dispatch (if needed)

When remote resolution isn't possible, we dispatch a technician to your location — quickly and without the runaround.

5

Resolution & Documentation

Every ticket is fully documented — what happened, what was done, and how to prevent it. Knowledge builds over time.

6

Follow-Up & Prevention

We follow up to confirm you're satisfied and analyze ticket patterns to proactively prevent the same issue from happening again.
